STORY

It started in a tiny kitchen in Poblenou, on a summer afternoon that was far too hot. We wanted something cold and fizzy, but every "healthy" drink in the fridge was either full of sugar or tasted like nothing at all.

So we started mixing. First the water: spring water from the Pyrenees, filtered slowly through rock for years before it ever reaches a can. Clean, soft and very cold.

Then the spark. We added a pinch of magnesium, potassium and sea salt, the same electrolytes your body loses on a long bike ride or a night of dancing. You can't taste them, but you can feel them.

The flavour comes from real fruit, cold-pressed into essences. No sweeteners, no colourings, nothing we can't pronounce. Blueberry and mint, blackcurrant and lime, blood orange and ginger, cucumber and yuzu.

And we put it all in aluminium, because a can can be recycled again and again, forever. Today OHMI is in the fridges of cafés, surf houses and night markets all over Barcelona. Still small, still fizzy, still made with the same simple idea: water that wakes you up.
